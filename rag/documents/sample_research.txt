Bitcoin Market Research

Bitcoin's price is affected by supply and demand, investor sentiment,
macroeconomic conditions, interest rates, liquidity, and regulatory news.

Bitcoin has a maximum supply of 21 million coins. Changes in demand can
affect its market price, but limited supply does not guarantee price increases.

Institutional investment, exchange-traded funds, exchange liquidity, and
large transactions can influence short-term market movements.

Regulatory announcements, security incidents, exchange failures, and
changes in market sentiment can cause substantial volatility.

Historical performance does not guarantee future returns.
This document is for educational research, not financial advice.
