Bitcoin Market Research

Bitcoin's price is affected by supply and demand, investor sentiment,
macroeconomic conditions, interest rates, liquidity, and regulatory news.

Bitcoin has a limited maximum supply of 21 million coins. Changes in demand
can affect its market price, but limited supply does not guarantee price increases.

Institutional investment, exchange-traded funds, exchange liquidity, and
large transactions can influence short-term market movements.

Higher interest rates may reduce demand for riskier assets, while expectations
of lower interest rates can improve risk appetite. These relationships are
not guaranteed and depend on broader market conditions.

Regulatory announcements, security incidents, exchange failures, and changes
in market sentiment can cause substantial volatility.

Historical performance does not guarantee future returns. This document is
for educational research and is not financial advice.
